# tntech_pcb_worksop

Files and references for a PCB design workshop at Tennessee Tech. The workshop will focus on designing and assembling a two-layer PCB for a simple Arduino-compatible STM32 development board, and will serve as a first-introduction to PCB design and assembly for students. Students are expected to have a familiarity with basic electrical engineering concepts such as Ohm's law, impedance, and basic circuit analysis, but no prior experience with PCB design is required. The workshop will be hands-on, and will culminate in students assembling a (hopefully) functional PCB that they will be able to take home with them.

Additional information can be found in the [documentation](documentation/main.md) for the workshop.
